FRAME BRIDGE 1.3.0 — EYE TRACKING VALIDATION
Tested on Windows x64, 28 October 2026 (local time).

Fixed native VRChat SDK beta compatibility:
- Detect /avatar/avatarfullfacetracking using OSCQuery, OSC T/F messages,
  nested OSC bundles, or local avatar configuration fallback.
- Send complete 102-byte /tracking/face/v1 OSC blob frames.
- Include separate left/right closure, eye widening and opposing pupil
  dilation/constriction. Unsupported brow inputs remain zero.
- Preserve the Frame runtime's normalized pupil range through Baballonia's
  nominal 2..8 mapping. These are not physical pupil diameter measurements.
- Pause older avatar parameters and gaze/combined-blink OSC in native mode;
  refresh every relevant legacy value when changing avatars or output modes.
